# SBE codecs

All encoders and decoders are generated by Real Logic SBE during Maven's generate-sources phase. Names ending in `Decoder` are reusable borrowed views, not records. Borrowed `getX()` getters return typed `Optional` containers cached at request setup; their contents are borrowed until the callback returns. Short value getters allocate immutable records with nested Optionals and exact BigDecimal prices, safe to retain after the callback.

Stream getters have matching `isXChanged()` and `isXCleared()` flags. Catalog getters have matching `forEachX(...)` accessors for repeated/subfield values. Short Catalog names are namespace-prefixed when ambiguous; `Class` becomes `Class_` to avoid Java's inherited `getClass()`.
